East Africa: fragrant, bright & shared

East Africa mixes highland traditions with Swahili coast flavours. In Ethiopia, sour injera is the foundation of every meal, while the coast layers cardamom, cloves and coconut, a legacy of Indian Ocean trade.

Staples

  • Teff, maize & sorghum
    for injera, ugali and porridges
  • Beans & lentils
    protein-rich stews and sides
  • Berbere, cardamom & cloves
    warm, layered spice blends
  • Coconut & plantain
    staples of the coast and lakes

Cooking techniques

  • Fermenting
    Teff batter ferments for days to make tangy injera.
  • Slow stewing
    Stews such as Ethiopian wat simmer for hours with niter kibbeh spiced butter.
  • Toasting whole spices
    Pilau starts with whole spices toasted in oil to release their aroma.

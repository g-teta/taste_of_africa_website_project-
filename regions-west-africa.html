West Africa: bold, smoky & soulful

West African food is bold, warm and generous. Meals are built around starchy staples such as rice, yam and cassava, served with thick soups and stews of tomato, peppers, okra and groundnuts, with heat from scotch bonnet peppers.

Staples

  • Rice, yam & cassava
    the base of almost every meal
  • Millet, sorghum & fonio
    ancient grains for porridges and sides
  • Palm oil & groundnuts
    for rich soups and sauces
  • Scotch bonnet, ginger & thyme
    the signature seasoning trio

Cooking techniques

  • One-pot cooking
    Rice and stews simmer in a single heavy pot so flavours blend, as in jollof.
  • Pounding
    Yam and cassava are pounded into smooth fufu to eat with soup.
  • Grilling
    Skewered suya meat is dusted with spicy peanut seasoning and grilled over open fire.

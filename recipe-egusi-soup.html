Rich Egusi Soup

A deeply savoury melon-seed soup enriched with leafy greens, palm oil and slow-cooked aromatics, served at tables across West Africa. It is traditionally eaten with pounded yam or fufu.

Preparation25 min
Cooking1 hr 20
DifficultyMedium
Serves4 people

What you’ll need

Ingredients

4
  • 1.5 cups ground egusi (melon seeds)
  • 500 g assorted meat
  • 2 cups chopped leafy greens
  • 1 red onion
  • 2 tbsp palm oil
  • 2 cups stock

Quantities scale automatically for 4 servings.

Take your time

The method

01Prepare the base

Heat the palm oil, soften the chopped onion, then stir in a paste of ground egusi and a splash of water until it forms soft curds.

02Simmer the soup

Add the stock and the cooked meat. Cover and simmer gently for 30 minutes, stirring now and then.

03Finish with greens

Fold in the chopped greens and cook for 10 minutes more. Season well and serve with pounded yam or fufu.
